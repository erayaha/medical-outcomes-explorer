# Agent Instructions — Medical Outcomes Explorer

## When to use this

Use Medical Outcomes Explorer when:
- You need comprehensive, risk-adjusted US hospital quality metrics (CMS 30-day mortality, unplanned readmissions, patient safety composites).
- You want to evaluate Medicare financial penalty risk, including Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program (HRRP) excess readmission ratios, Hospital-Acquired Condition Reduction Program (HACRP) infection standardized ratios (CLABSI, CAUTI, SSI, CDI, MRSA), and Hospital Value-Based Purchasing (HVBP) domain baselines.
- You are conducting post-market surveillance on FDA medical devices (510(k) clearances, MAUDE adverse events, Class I/II/III recalls).
- You are qualifying clinical trial investigation sites based on academic research tiering, bed capacity, and specialty surgical volumes.
- You need open, reproducible healthcare datasets exported in structured JSON and CSV formats.

## Best-fit use cases

1. **Hospital Quality & Financial Risk Analysis**: Benchmark facilities against state and national averages to model HRRP and HACRP payment adjustments.
2. **MedTech Post-Market Intelligence**: Track MAUDE death, injury, and malfunction event signals and recall timelines across 8 therapeutic areas.
3. **Clinical Trial Site Selection**: Profile Tier 1 Academic Medical Centers for therapeutic trial readiness.
4. **Epidemiological Research**: Ingest clean, structured CMS and FDA data snapshots with full federal dataset provenance.
